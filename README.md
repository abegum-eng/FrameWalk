# FrameWalk

An app designed to help you learn photography and never run out of things to photograph, all while taking relaxing walks through your community.

FrameWalk/

## Goals

Users will be able to develop their photography skills, explore their surrounding area, and see the change in their environment through routes/locations around Seattle with photography prompts, lesson suggestions, and camera tools.

Whether it be struggling beginners or people stuck at a plateau, lack of progress and inspiration can demotivate, kill consistency, and vice versa. We don’t want users to be stuck wishing to be better and feeling like they don’t have the discipline, inspiration, or resources to try. 

Since people have their typical routes around school, work, and their general neighborhood, new places and scenery lie waiting to be discovered. Offering users a new, specific path with curated photo prompts will let them literally see the space through a new lens.

Skill level can change just as quickly as the seasons, and we want users to reflect on a detailed catalog of their development along with their ever-changing environment.
